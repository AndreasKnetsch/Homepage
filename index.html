Research

I work at the intersection of corporate finance and financial accounting. Most of my research asks what managerial language reveals, drawing on text sources such as the rhetoric of earnings conference calls, CEO television interviews after earnings announcements, reorganization plans filed in Chapter 11, or the sustainability disclosures of firms and funds.
